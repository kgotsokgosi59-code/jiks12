## 2. Terms of use on this site

1. **Every file must be licensed for web use before it is uploaded.** Buying or
   downloading a track for personal listening does **not** grant the right to use
   it as background music on a website.
2. **Two rights are normally needed for a music track** — the recording (usually
   held by the record label) and the song itself (held by the composers'
   publisher). Royalty-free library subscriptions cover both; a direct deal means
   clearing both sides.
3. **Nothing is edited, remixed or synchronised to video** without the licence
   explicitly allowing it.
4. **Credits stay on the site** for as long as the music is used.
5. **Remove a track immediately** if its licence expires or is withdrawn.

## 5. Not licensed — what we do instead

Until a track is cleared, the site links the official streaming playlist rather
than hosting the audio. Streaming services pay the rights holders through their
own licences; embedding their official player is permitted and is the safe way to
put this music in front of visitors.
